CRUISING ELEVATED

Avalon Waterways is rewriting the rules of river cruising. With forward-thinking design, immersive experiences, and unapologetic originality, we're flipping the script on what small-ship cruising can be—and who it's for.

From signature Panorama Suites® with the industry's only Open-Air Balconies® to choose-your-adventure excursions and vibrant after-dark programming, Avalon sails beyond the ordinary.

Consistently recognized as the highest-rated river cruise line, Avalon has redefined what award-winning should look like. Our Suite Ships® are celebrated because they're designed around you: more space, more choice, more personalization. With room to breathe and freedom to explore, every journey becomes as story-worthy as the destinations themselves.

ITS A SHORE THING WITH AVALON

Cruise with the Whim in Your Sails

No one does river cruising like this. With Avalon Choice® Excursions, stepping off the ship is just the beginning. Explore your way with Classic, Discovery and Active experiences – whether that means VIP access to landmarks, stirring up local specialties, or sightseeing by kayak or e-bike.


With up to 28 included excursions per cruise and small groups that go deeper, Avalon offers more included choices than any other river cruise line. And for those who prefer to roam freely, our Adventure Center is stocked with complimentary bikes, e-bikes, maps, and gear – turning free time into choose-your-own-adventure discoveries.


With Avalon, you’re free to follow your passions, set your own pace, and discover the world your way.

THE AVALON ADVANTAGE: SMALL SHIPS. BIG MOMENTS.

SMALLER SHIPS

Aboard our award-winning Suite Ships, “crowd pleasing” is not in our vocabulary. Instead, we set our sights on pleasing you with thoroughly modern, best-in-class ships averaging just 150 guests–or less. Smaller is a bigger idea in giving you the space, the time, and the personalized attention you deserve. The days of crowded cruising? That ship has sailed!

BIGGER EXPERIENCES

Less crowds. More wow! On shore, Avalon keeps things personal with smaller excursion groups. That means front-row access to landmarks, richer connections with Local Guides, and deeper dives into culture, history, and hidden corners. We don’t just check the boxes. Avalon unlocks larger-than-life moments in smaller, smarter, and more intentional ways.

ALL THIS. ONLY AVALON.

LET'S DISH

Dining without rules. Choose when, where and how you eat. Enjoy breakfast with a view, local flavors, seasonal menus, and freshly prepared meals made just for you.

RAISE A GLASS

Complimentary wine, beer and soft drinks with meals. Signature cocktails, coffee, tea and fresh beverages keep the celebration going throughout your journey.

AFTER DARK

Live music, rooftop cocktails, karaoke, trivia and exciting evening entertainment bring every night to life onboard and during overnight city stays.

MIND. BODY. RIVER.

Stay fit. Stay centered. Stay you. From yoga on the Sky Deck and Adventure Host activities to biking, hiking and fitness centers, Avalon offers wellness experiences throughout your journey.

FREEDOM FLOWS HERE

Travel isn't one-size-fits-all. Choose guided excursions or explore independently with help from your Adventure Host. Every journey is designed around your travel style.

AT YOUR SERVICE

Our dedicated crew delivers exceptional service from the moment you board until your journey ends. Every detail is handled with care to make your vacation unforgettable.
